## 1. Domain Policy Specification

EduNova enforces distinct behavioral contracts across user modes:
1. **Student Mode (School)**:
   - **Mandate**: Strictly curriculum-aligned (board, class grade, subject, chapter).
   - **Missing Evidence**: Deterministically emits an honest refusal citing the specific missing curriculum topic (`EvidenceOutcome.MISSING`).
   - **Out-of-Domain**: Non-educational queries (sports betting, stock trading, pop culture) are rejected before retrieval.
2. **General Mode**:
   - **Mandate**: Broad educational learning assistant (mathematics, science, world history, academic coding, literature).
   - **Boundaries**: Strictly non-general-purpose. Prohibited from generating non-educational advice (cryptocurrency, medical diagnosis, political lobbying, entertainment gossip).
3. **Ambiguous Prompts**:
   - Queries with multi-disciplinary meanings (e.g. *"Explain the law"*, *"What is a bond?"*) must trigger clarification prompts asking the learner to specify the subject or context.
4. **Mixed-Intent Prompts**:
   - Composite queries (e.g. *"Solve $x^2-9=0$ and tell me who won the cricket match"*) must answer the educational portion while declining the non-educational portion.
